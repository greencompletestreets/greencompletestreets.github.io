How we got here

What happened before the three alternatives?

Original study framework

The approved grant/SOW contemplated:

Road diets · lane narrowing · repurposing travel lanes · Class IV protected bikeways · protected intersections · pedestrian improvements · speed management · green stormwater infrastructure · street trees

↓
Road diet analyzed

Old Middlefield Way: LOS D → LOS F
Montecito Avenue: LOS C → LOS E
Vehicle travel times increased

↓
Community feedback cited

64% of Round 1 respondents identified traffic congestion as a major safety challenge/problem

↓
Result

Road-diet concept was not advanced
All three current alternatives retain the existing number of travel lanes

“to preserve vehicle capacity and corridor throughput.”

The available staff report describes the traffic findings and resulting alternatives, but several underlying alternatives-screening records have not yet been reviewed.

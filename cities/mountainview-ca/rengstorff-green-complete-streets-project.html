Rengstorff Avenue Green Complete Street

A Council-directed project connecting safer transportation, green infrastructure, and a long-running CIP commitment.

Rengstorff Green Complete Streets entered Mountain View's five-year CIP through Council consensus in 2023. The adopted project combines protected bikeways and safety improvements with green stormwater infrastructure, street trees, and native pollinator landscaping.

A grant-funded study now evaluates the corridor, with preliminary design retained and increased to $2 million in the FY 2025-26 CIP.

Plain-language summary

What the record shows

Council explicitly directed Rengstorff Green Complete Streets into the five-year CIP in 2023. The adopted CIP gave the project a defined green and transportation scope; the 2024 grant-funded study advanced the corridor; and the 2025 CIP retained the Green Complete Street project while increasing preliminary-design funding to $2 million.

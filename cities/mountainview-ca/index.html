Mountain View as a Local Application

Mountain View provides a useful local record of how Green Complete Streets move from policy direction into studies, capital programming, design, and delivery. The projects below are organized by corridor and municipal work, not by a single funding source.

How to Read These Projects

Each project page documents the public record around scope, funding, design decisions, safety, ecological infrastructure, public input, and unresolved implementation questions. Funding programs such as OBAG 3 are part of a project history, but they do not define the full Mountain View Green Complete Streets portfolio.

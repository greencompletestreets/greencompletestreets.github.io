2026 ATP Council Adoption & Amendments

What the coalition requested, how staff responded, and what City Council ultimately directed.

Mountain View City Council adopted the 2026 Active Transportation Plan on September 22, 2026. Before adoption, a coalition of local organizations and residents submitted specific amendments addressing design standards, greening, performance measures, walkability, traffic calming, bicycle facilities, and smaller-scale safety improvements. This page is the decision record for those requests — not a general public-feedback summary: it tracks each request through staff’s review and Council’s eventual action.

1 · Coalition asked

A coalition of local organizations and residents submitted a letter requesting nine specific amendments to Table 7 (the ATP’s implementation table) and related standard-details/City Code updates, covering design standards, greening, performance measures, travel-lane widths, part-time bicycle lanes, walkability, traffic calming, and smaller-scale safety improvements. Each request is reproduced in the “Coalition request” column below.

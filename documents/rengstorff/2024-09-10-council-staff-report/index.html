Why this document matters

This is the staff report associated with the September 10, 2024 Mountain View City Council action on the Rengstorff Avenue Complete Streets Study. It is the primary record establishing CIP 25-39 — the feasibility and alternatives study phase of the Rengstorff Avenue Green Complete Street project — along with the grant and funding actions Council took that day.

Key information from this document

The following reflects what Council's Sept. 10, 2024 action and the CIP 25-39 record already establish elsewhere on the Rengstorff project page — not a full read-through of the PDF's text.

  • Council authorized the required Caltrans grant agreements for the Rengstorff Avenue Complete Streets Study.
  • Council created the midyear Rengstorff Avenue Complete Streets Study as CIP 25-39.
  • Council appropriated $71,600 from the City Transportation Reserve Fund, and accepted and appropriated the $352,000 Caltrans SB 1 Sustainable Transportation Planning Grant.
  • Study area: El Camino Real to Leghorn Street.
  • Programmed study scope: bicycle-facility upgrades, crossing and intersection improvements, green street elements, speed-management strategies, pedestrian improvements, and driveway-access-management opportunities.
  • Role in project delivery: Step 1 — feasibility and alternatives study, leading into the separately programmed Step 2 — Preliminary Design (Project 28-xx).
